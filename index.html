Our Approach

Why ReadEase?

A sentence that confuses a 7-year-old is perfectly normal for a college student. ReadEase understands that readability is personal — it accounts for your age and grade level before judging difficulty.

We run six industry-standard readability formulas simultaneously and calibrate the results against your profile for the most accurate picture possible.

  • ✓ 6 readability formulas run in parallel
  • ✓ Age-calibrated difficulty scoring
  • ✓ Hardest sentences extracted & ranked
  • ✓ Plain-English explanations on demand
  • ✓ Text-to-speech for every sentence
  • ✓ Analysis history saved locally
🧮
6 Formulas, 1 Score
We run Flesch, Flesch-Kincaid, Gunning Fog, SMOG, Coleman-Liau & ARI simultaneously and combine them into one easy-to-read verdict.
🎯
Calibrated to Your Age
A Grade 8 text is easy for a college student but challenging for a 10-year-old. We adjust every result against your exact reading level.
🔊
Listen & Understand
Every sentence can be read aloud at a pace suited to your age. Great for auditory learners, ESL students, or anyone who prefers to listen.
💡
Plain-English Breakdowns
Complex sentences get rewritten into simple language with clear explanations of what made them difficult in the first place.
